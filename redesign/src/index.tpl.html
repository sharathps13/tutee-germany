      06Your pathway

      From student to settled.

      We map your route to a German career from day one, so every choice you make as a student points somewhere.

      1. 1
        While you study

        Student residence permit

        Complete your program. We help you pick courses, internships and German classes that lead into a German career.

      2. 2
        After graduation

        18-month job-seeker permit

        Graduates can stay up to 18 months to find a job matching their degree, and may work any job meanwhile.

      3. 3
        Qualified work

        EU Blue Card or skilled-worker permit

        Move into qualified employment. The Blue Card salary threshold is lower for shortage occupations and recent graduates.

      4. 4
        Long term

        Permanent settlement

        Apply for a settlement permit (Niederlassungserlaubnis) after as little as 21 months on a Blue Card with B1 German.

      Relocating with family?

      We'll be straight with you.

      Bringing family on a student permit is possible only with enough funds and housing for everyone, which is hard on a student budget. The routes open up once you're working, and we plan that sequence with you from the start.

      While studying
      Family reunion only in limited cases; most families visit on a Schengen visa.
      On an EU Blue Card
      Spouse and children can join, with no German test for the spouse, who may also work.
